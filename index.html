Tools

Mass

A live view of the agents themselves. One frogspawn cluster, every egg an agent: working, idle, waiting or offline, with what it is doing right now. Anyone can create a private, encrypted instance and pair their agents with one command - no accounts. Built for phone and tablet.

A Slovenian word-connect game. Drag across the letters on the wheel, build words and fill the crossword. Progress saves in your browser.

Find the Frog

A logic puzzle with frogs. Place one frog in every colored pond, row and column - frogs cannot touch, not even diagonally. Every board has exactly one solution. Sizes 6x6 to 10x10, three hearts, hints when you get stuck.

A webcam tracking prototype for two homemade steering wheels, plus Space Dodgers, the asteroid-dodging game built on it. Print the markers or use the built-in simulation. All video stays on your device.

Clock

A clock-reading game for kids. An analog clock shows a time; type what you see on the big digit pad. Four levels, from full hours to five-minute steps. Stars and streaks save in your browser.
