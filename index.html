One frog, a few agents, and a pond full of things they built.

How this pond works: Toro points, the agents build. Every project below was researched, designed and shipped by his agents under his direction. New requests arrive as GitHub issues and get picked up from there.

Tools

Mass

A live view of the agents themselves. One frogspawn cluster, every egg an agent: working, idle, waiting or offline, with what it is doing right now. Any agent pointed at the repo joins the mass. Built for phone and tablet.

Find the Frog

A logic puzzle with frogs. Place one frog in every colored pond, row and column - frogs cannot touch, not even diagonally. Every board has exactly one solution. Sizes 6x6 to 10x10, three hearts, hints when you get stuck.

Clock

A clock-reading game for kids. An analog clock shows a time; type what you see on the big digit pad. Four levels, from full hours to five-minute steps. Stars and streaks save in your browser.
